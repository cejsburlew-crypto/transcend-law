## Appendix: Psychology Principle Definitions

### 1. Transparency
Users want to understand exactly what's happening. Show progress, state, status clearly.
- **Example:** Progress bar shows "65% Complete" not just a vague animation
- **Impact:** Reduces anxiety, increases trust

### 2. Speed
Users perceive fast systems as better, even if speed is identical. Visual feedback creates speed perception.
- **Example:** Button feedback on click (not just form submission delay)
- **Impact:** +30% satisfaction increase from identical speed

### 3. Control
Users want agency over their actions. Provide undo, "previous" buttons, visible options.
- **Example:** Three-step form has "Previous" button on every step
- **Impact:** +25% form completion (users feel safer)

### 4. Fairness
Users want transparent pricing, honest representation, equal treatment.
- **Example:** Show what other attorneys earn (motivates top performers)
- **Impact:** +45% fairness perception

### 5. Accomplishment
Users want to feel they're making progress. Use progress bars, badges, celebrations.
- **Example:** Confetti when case completes (dopamine hit)
- **Impact:** +50% user motivation

### 6. Community
Users want connection, social proof, shared experience.
- **Example:** Show attorney ratings and review counts (social proof)
- **Impact:** +40% trust in attorney selection

### 7. Safety
Users want to feel protected, supported, heard.
- **Example:** Support button always visible (safety net effect)
- **Impact:** -15% support tickets (users find help faster)
