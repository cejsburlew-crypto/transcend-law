## 💰 REVENUE MODEL EXPLAINED

### Notary Service (Tier 1)
- **When Transcend Law Earns:** Per job completed (amount TBD)
- **Who Pays:** Notary pays fee from their earnings
- **How:** Built into per-job compensation model

### Contract-Based Services (Tier 2 - All 19 other services)
- **When Transcend Law Earns:** When client ACCEPTS contract
  - Not when request is made
  - Not when work is completed
  - **Exactly when:** Contract terms are accepted by both parties
  
- **How Much:** 15% of agreed service rate
  - Example: $1,000 agreed rate = $150 referral fee to Transcend Law
  
- **Who Pays:** Service provider pays referral fee
  
- **Payment Flow:**
  1. Provider proposes terms ($1,000)
  2. Client accepts ($1,000 rate)
  3. Transcend Law captures $150 (15% referral fee)
  4. Provider delivers service
  5. Client pays provider $1,000 (directly, not through Transcend)
  6. Provider keeps full $1,000
