# 💼 B2B Subscription Tiers & Marketing Structure

**Model:** LinkedIn Premium + Marketplace Sales Structure  
**Revenue Stream:** Tier subscriptions + Featured listings  
**Target:** Company-to-Company relationships (NOT individual spam)

---

## Core Principle: B2B NOT B2C SPAM

**✅ ALLOWED:** Firm Admin → Firm Admin outreach
- Company Professional Network builds connections
- Admin-to-admin messaging (no individual spamming)
- Marketing to decision makers

**❌ NOT ALLOWED:** Blasting individual lawyers
- No DM spam to lawyers
- No unsolicited emails to paralegals
- No bulk messaging bypass

---

## Subscription Tier Structure

### TIER 1: FREE (Baseline Marketplace Presence)

**Cost:** $0/month  
**Commitment:** Free  

**Features:**
- Basic company profile (name, location, services offered, contact)
- Searchable in marketplace
- 0% featured visibility
- No direct messaging
- Max 5 service categories
- Up to 5 team members listed
- Basic analytics (view count only)
- Email leads only (inquiries to company email)

**Good for:** Solo practitioners, trying it out, part-time service providers

---

### TIER 2: STARTER (Entry-Level Growth)

**Cost:** $49/month ($588/year)  
**Commitment:** Monthly, cancel anytime  
**Billing:** Monthly or annual (save 15% annually)

**Features:**
- Enhanced company profile
- Logo/branding uploads
- Service portfolio (case studies, testimonials)
- Website link & social profiles
- LinkedIn-style endorsements
- 10% featured visibility (appears 10% of the time in marketplace searches)
- Basic company outreach:
  - Can connect with up to 5 companies/month
  - Basic company message (not to individuals)
  - Connection request to company profiles
- Team profiles (up to 10 members)
- Basic analytics:
  - Monthly profile views
  - Search impressions
  - Listing clicks
- Lead notifications (email)

**Good for:** Small firms, established solo practitioners, growing practices

---

### TIER 3: PROFESSIONAL (Active Growth)

**Cost:** $149/month ($1,788/year)  
**Commitment:** Monthly, cancel anytime  
**Billing:** Monthly or annual (save 15% annually)

**Features:**
- All Starter features, plus:
- 40% featured visibility (premium placement)
- Featured badge on profile
- Company outreach:
  - Can connect with up to 20 companies/month
  - Premium company messages (with credentials display)
  - Direct messaging with verified firm admins
  - Bulk outreach to companies with custom messaging
- Featured listing options:
  - Featured in specialty search (additional $29/month or included)
  - Featured in location search (additional $29/month or included)
- Team profiles (up to 25 members)
- Advanced analytics:
  - Detailed traffic sources
  - Conversion funnel (views → messages → hires)
  - Competitor benchmarking
- Marketing materials download
- Monthly performance report
- Priority support (email, 24hr response)
- CRM integration (Salesforce, HubSpot)

**Good for:** Mid-size firms, active marketers, high-volume service providers

---

### TIER 4: ENTERPRISE (Maximum Reach)

**Cost:** Custom pricing starting at $499/month  
**Commitment:** Annual contract  
**Billing:** Monthly billing + volume discounts

**Features:**
- All Professional features, plus:
- 80% featured visibility (top of search results)
- Platinum badge (premium indicator)
- Premium company outreach:
  - Unlimited company connections/month
  - Premium messaging with credential verification
  - Bulk campaigns (1000+ companies)
  - Personalized outreach automation
  - Response tracking & follow-up reminders
- Unlimited featured listings in all specialties
- Showcase landing page (custom subdomain)
- Team profiles (unlimited)
- Custom branding options
- Enterprise analytics:
  - Real-time conversion tracking
  - Custom report generation
  - ROI calculator
  - Predictive analytics
- White-glove onboarding
- Dedicated account manager
- Quarterly business reviews
- Advanced integrations:
  - Salesforce
  - HubSpot

## Marketplace Discovery Mechanics (LinkedIn-Style)

### Search Algorithm

**Organic Search Ranking (Free Visibility):**
1. Profile completeness (80-100%)
2. Service match to search query
3. Location match
4. Recent activity/updates
5. Reviews/ratings
6. Response time

**Featured Visibility Boost:**
- Featured users appear higher in results
- Different featured sections for different tiers
- Sponsored badge shows they're highlighted

### Category Browsing

**Directory Views:**
- By Service Type (Lawyer, Notary, Mediator, etc.)
- By Location (State/City)
- By Practice Area (Corporate, Family, Tax, etc.)
- By Company Size
- By Ratings/Reviews
- By Price Range

## Fraud & Abuse Prevention

**Verification System:**
- Email verification required
- Phone verification (Professional+)
- Company registration verification
- License verification (for licensed services)
- Badge system for verified accounts

**Messaging Safeguards:**
- No spam detection system
- Opt-out from messaging
- Report inappropriate contact
- Admin review of disputes
- Suspension for violations

**Rate Limiting:**
- Free tier: 5 connections/month
- Starter: 5 connections/month
- Professional: 20 connections/month
- Enterprise: Unlimited

## Competitive Positioning

**vs. LinkedIn:**
- More specialized for legal services
- Better filtering for practice areas
- Jurisdiction-specific searches
- Legal-specific verification
- Direct booking integration

**vs. Avvo:**
- Better B2B relationships
- Subscription marketing model
- Less focused on consumer reviews
- More comprehensive tool integration

**vs. LegalMatch:**
- Subscription not just leads
- Company-level relationships
- Professional network building
