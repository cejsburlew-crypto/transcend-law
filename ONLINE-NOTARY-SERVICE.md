## OVERVIEW

**Online Notary Service** is a premium notarization platform integrated into TRANSCEND LAW that allows:

- 📝 **Notaries** to create profiles, manage requests, and earn $30+ per stamped page
- 👤 **Clients** to access notarized documents within their case workflow
- ⚖️ **Attorneys** to request notarizations directly in case management
- 🎓 **SMEs** to include notarized documents in expert consultations
- 🚚 **Vehicle Registration** to notarize title transfers and registrations
- 💼 **All parties** to share documents across the integrated workflow

**Key Innovation:** Notarized documents are stored in the client case and accessible to all authorized parties (attorneys, SMEs, other service providers).
