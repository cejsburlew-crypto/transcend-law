Example: `03-125694 Longley Way Safety Plan`

## Required SSP components (CFC §3303.1.1 / BU 24-05)

1. Name and contact information of **site safety director**
2. Documentation of training of site safety director and **fire watch** personnel
3. Procedures for **reporting emergencies**
4. **Fire department vehicle access routes**
5. Locations of **fire protection equipment** (extinguishers, standpipes, FDCs, hydrants)
6. **Smoking and cooking** policies and designated areas
7. **Temporary heating** equipment location and safety
8. **Hot work** plan (welding, roofing, etc.)
9. Plans for control of **combustible waste**
10. Storage and use of **flammable/combustible liquids** and hazardous materials
11. Provisions for **site security**
12. **Changes** that affect this plan
13. Other **site-specific information** requested by the local fire authority (LFA)

## Typical package contents (Longley Way example)

- Fire access / site plan sheet (LFA coordination)
- BU 24-05 bulletin
- Contractor Injury & Illness Prevention Program (IIPP)
- Emergency / heat illness / job-specific safety procedures

The app captures structured data for items 1–13 plus site identifiers, then exports or assembles the full PDF package separately.
