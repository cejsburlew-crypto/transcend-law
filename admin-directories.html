Body:

Dear Secretary of State Records Division,

We are TRANSCEND LAW, a legal services technology platform. We are building a comprehensive national notary registry to connect licensed notaries with clients seeking professional notarization services.

We request a bulk export of all currently active notary public licenses for [STATE], including:
• Full names
• License/commission numbers
• Commission expiration dates
• County/city of residence
• Contact email/phone (if available)

This data will be used only for professional matching and outreach to notaries. We will maintain confidentiality and comply with all state privacy requirements.

Please advise on:
1. Cost (if applicable)
2. Format (CSV, Excel, XML)
3. Delivery timeline
4. Update frequency for monthly/quarterly refreshes
