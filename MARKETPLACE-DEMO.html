⚖️ Attorney Licensing

Verify state bar licenses for all attorneys. Display public info from state bar in client communications.

💳 Automatic Billing

Automatic recurring charges. Grace periods. Automatic suspension after 7 days. Data preservation for 30 days.

📥 Smart Lead Routing

3 routing types: specific firm, multiple firms (first-wins), or open recommendation to all matching firms.

💬 In-App Messaging Only

No email auto-replies. All communication through Transcend platform. Keeps clients engaged with the platform.

📊 Real-Time Analytics

For each firm: response time, acceptance rate, rating. For you: MRR, churn, lead volume, conversion rates.

🔐 Data Isolation

Multi-tenant architecture. Each firm sees only their own leads. Complete data separation. GDPR/CCPA compliant.

⭐ Client Reviews

After case closes, clients rate attorneys. Ratings visible to future clients. Incentivizes quality service.

📱 Public Directory

Firms listed publicly by practice area and location. Searchable. Ratings visible. Drives organic lead generation.

🧑‍⚖️ Co-Counsel Support

Multiple licensed attorneys per case. Lead attorney assigns co-counsel. Case staff (non-attorneys) with permissions.

💰 Revenue Model

$99-$999/month subscription. Optional 10% commission on cases. 90% gross margin at 300 firms. $100K+ MRR possible.

🌍 Competitive Edge

vs Avvo: leads not just directory. vs TheLaw: digital-first, no phone calls. vs Justia: structured matching.

🚀 Scale Ready

Multi-tenant database. Load balancing ready. Handles 1000s of firms. API-first architecture. White-label capable.
