# TRANSCEND LAW - TWO-TIER SERVICE MODEL

## Overview

The platform now supports two distinct service delivery models tailored to different service types:

### 🔏 **TIER 1: NOTARY SERVICE - REAL-TIME JOB BOARD**
- 24/7 mobile on-demand service
- Real-time job matching
- Immediate availability selection
- Instant client matching

### 💼 **TIER 2: ALL OTHER SERVICES - CONTRACT NEGOTIATION**
- Professional services (Lawyer, Consultant, Paralegal, etc.)
- Contract-based engagement
- Term negotiation between provider and client
- Referral fee model for Transcend Law

---

## 🔏 TIER 1: NOTARY JOB BOARD SYSTEM

### How It Works

**For Notaries (Service Providers):**

1. **Set Availability**
   - Select availability level from Job Board:
     - 🟢 **Immediately** - Get matched with urgent jobs right now
     - 🟡 **In 5 minutes** - Available soon
     - 🟡 **In 15 minutes** - Short notice
     - 🟠 **In 30 minutes** - Standard availability
     - ⚫ **Offline** - Not taking jobs

2. **View Available Jobs**
   - See real-time job board with available requests
   - Jobs sorted by urgency, proximity, and compensation
   - Job details show:
     - Client name
     - Service type (Loan Signing, Acknowledgment, Jurat, Apostille, etc.)
     - Location (distance in miles)
     - Estimated duration
     - Urgency level with color coding
     - Compensation amount ($)
     - Project description

3. **Accept Jobs**
   - Click "Accept Job" to claim a request
   - Confirmation shows client they'll have notary in X minutes
   - Client receives notification with your ETA

4. **Complete Job**
   - When job is complete, mark as "Completed"
   - Automatically eligible for payment
   - Option to add notes/invoice

**For Clients Requesting Notary:**

1. **Submit Request**
   - Fill out notary intake form
   - Specify service type needed
   - Upload documents if applicable
   - Pay deposit/full amount (depends on terms)

2. **See Available Notaries**
   - See list of notaries currently available
   - Filter by availability window:
     - "Someone available now"
     - "Someone in 5 minutes"
     - "Someone in 15 minutes"
     - "Someone in 30 minutes"
   - Choose preferred availability window or pick specific notary

3. **Automatic Matching (If "Immediately")**
   - If client selects "immediately available"
   - System matches with first available notary in that category
   - Notary receives job offer
   - Once accepted, client gets notary details and ETA

4. **Receive Service**
   - Notary arrives at specified time
   - Service completed
   - Documents returned
   - Pay balance if applicable

### Key Features

**Real-Time Updates:**
- Notaries see jobs in real-time as they're posted
- Jobs disappear when claimed by another notary
- ETA counters update live

**Rating System:**
- Clients rate notaries after job completion
- Affects notary visibility in future job matching
- High-rated notaries get matched faster

**Completion Tracking:**
- Total jobs completed visible on profile
- Acceptance rate shows reliability
- Earnings summary by job type

**Notifications:**
- Job alerts for newly posted requests
- Client confirmations when job accepted
- Completion confirmations

## 💼 TIER 2: CONTRACT NEGOTIATION SYSTEM (ALL OTHER SERVICES)

### How It Works

**For Service Providers (Lawyers, Consultants, Investigators, etc.):**

1. **View Incoming Requests**
   - Three tabs: Pending, Negotiating, Accepted
   - Each request shows:
     - Client name and budget
     - Service type needed
     - Project description
     - When request was posted

2. **Review & Respond**
   - Read client's project details
   - Send messages to ask clarifying questions
   - Have back-and-forth conversation

3. **Propose Terms**
   - Click "Propose Terms" to send formal proposal
   - Specify:
     - **Scope:** What you'll deliver
     - **Rate:** Your fee for this project
     - **Timeline:** How long it will take
     - **Payment Terms:** Upfront, milestone-based, or on completion
   - Client can accept or negotiate

4. **Negotiate Terms**
   - Client responds to your proposal
   - Can accept, counter-offer, or decline
   - Continue messaging until agreement

5. **Accept Contract**
   - Once terms are agreed, click "Accept Contract"
   - Transcend Law captures referral fee at this point
   - You're now engaged with the client
   - Begin delivering service

6. **Complete & Get Paid**
   - Complete the agreed-upon work
   - Client pays you directly for your services
   - Transcend Law's referral fee was collected when client accepted

### Revenue Model

**When Does Transcend Law Earn?**

Transcend Law collects a referral fee when:
- ✅ Client **ACCEPTS** the service provider (not when they request)
- Service provider and client have negotiated and agreed to terms
- Contract is accepted by the provider

**How Much?**

- **Referral Fee:** 15% of the agreed service rate
- **Example:** Provider proposes $1,000 rate
  - Transcend Law gets: $150 (15% referral fee)
  - Service provider keeps: Full $1,000 from client
  - Client pays: $1,000 to provider (Transcend's fee is built into our economics, not added)

**Who Pays What?**

1. **Service Provider** → Transcend Law: 15% referral fee (on acceptance)
2. **Client** → Service Provider: Full agreed service rate (negotiated in contract)
3. **Service Provider** → Client: Delivers services

**Note:** This is a referral model. Transcend Law facilitates the connection; the provider and client conduct business directly.

### Contract Negotiation Features

**Messaging System:**
- Real-time client-provider communication
- Message history preserved in contract thread
- Timestamps and read receipts

**Term Proposals:**
- Formal proposal templates
- Scope, rate, timeline, payment terms
- Client can view full proposal in contract view

**Request Management:**
- Pending: New requests awaiting response
- Negotiating: Active discussions happening
- Accepted: Completed contracts ready to work

**Status Tracking:**
- Know which contracts are negotiating
- Know which are accepted and ready
- Track completion of accepted contracts

## 💰 EARNING EXAMPLES

### Notary Example

**Job Posted:**
- Client needs loan signing notarization
- Location: 2.3 miles away
- Estimated duration: 45 minutes
- Posted compensation: $150

**Notary Flow:**
1. Notary sets availability to "Immediately" on job board
2. Job appears in real-time feed
3. Notary accepts job
4. Client is notified notary is arriving in 5 minutes
5. Notary travels, arrives, completes job (45 min)
6. Notary marks job complete
7. Client pays $150
8. Notary receives $150 (minus any platform fee if applicable)
9. Transcend Law referral fee: TBD (market-based)

**Key Point:** Notary gets paid directly by client for completed job.

### Contract Negotiation Example

**Request Posted:**
- Client: ABC Corp
- Service: Contract Reviewer
- Budget: $1,500
- Description: Review vendor contract for software services

**Provider Flow:**
1. Provider sees pending request
2. Provider asks questions via messaging
3. Client responds with details
4. Provider proposes terms:
   - Scope: Full contract review, mark-up, negotiation notes
   - Rate: $1,200
   - Timeline: 3 business days
   - Payment: 50% upfront, 50% on delivery

5. Client accepts proposal
   - **Transcend Law immediately collects referral fee:** $1,200 × 15% = $180
   - **Provider will earn:** $1,200 from client
   - **Client will pay:** $1,200 to provider directly

6. Provider begins work
7. Provider completes review and sends to client
8. Client pays provider $1,200 (direct payment)
9. Provider keeps full $1,200

**Key Difference from Notary:**
- Transcend Law earns referral fee when contract is ACCEPTED (before work starts)
- Service provider and client handle payment directly
- Provider keeps 100% of agreed rate; Transcend Law already got referral fee
